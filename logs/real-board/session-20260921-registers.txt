Real-board session, 2026-09-21
Board: EUROCOM-17, rev 1.M, serial 187562, dual 68040, 32 MB, no video fitted.
Access: serial console bridged to MQTT by smolmqtt's serial2mqtt
        (serial2mqtt -b 9600 -c 8N1 /dev/ttyUSB1 <broker> m68k/e17),
        driven with scripts/e17con.py.  All RMON commands below are read-only.

VIC register n is the low byte of the long at fec01000 + n*4 (byte lane 3).
Decoded, as RMON leaves it:

    reg  0- 7  (+03..+1f)  f8 f9 fa fb fc fd fe ff   VME IRQ1-7 vector regs
    reg  9     (+27) LICR1 = 88   keyboard      masked (bit7)
    reg 10     (+2b) LICR2 = 37   VIC tick      ENABLED, VIC-vectored (bit4),
                                                sense 0x30, CPU level 7
    reg 11     (+2f) LICR3 = 88   SCSI          masked
    reg 12     (+33) LICR4 = 88   video frame   masked
    reg 13     (+37) LICR5 = 88   LEB           masked
    reg 14     (+3b) LICR6 = 88   CD2401+CIOs   masked
    reg 15     (+3f) LICR7 = 88   ILACC         masked
    reg 17     (+47) ICMSICR  = 7f   ICMS0 masked (bit4), group IPL 7
    reg 20     (+53) ICMSIVBR = 1f
    reg 21     (+57) LIVBR    = 40   <-- confirms vector = 0x40 | LIRQn
    reg 23     (+5f) ICFSR    = 00   no interprocessor switch set
    reg 40     (+a3) transfer time-out = 44
    reg 48     (+c3) SSCR0    = d2   tick timer enabled, bits 7:6 = 11
    reg 49/50/51 (+c7/+cb/+cf) = 62 16 62

So out of RMON the ONLY unmasked local interrupt is the VIC's own tick timer,
at CPU level 7, 100 Hz.  (U-Boot turns it off again in cpu_init_r.)

reg 0x00 = fec00000 (own base), 0xa4 = f0000000, 0xa8 = 0000528b.
ROM bank fe800000/fff00000/0a03 and SRAM bank fea00000/fff00000/0a04 confirm
the modelled map.

=============================================================================
System CIO ports  --  db fec30000 4
=============================================================================
fec30000 : ff 01 89 94
    +0 port C  = ff
    +1 port B  = 01   DIP switches: low nibble 1 = "load the saved NVRAM
                      configuration" (not a ROM profile, no autostart)
    +2 port A  = 89   PA7 = 1 -> watchdog indicator NOT lit *at this moment*.
                      Note this proves little: RMON pets the watchdog, and any
                      write to $FEC5.0000 clears the indicator.  The kernel's
                      breadcrumb is the reliable "was it a watchdog reset".
    +3 control = 94   (MCCR).  Do not make a habit of reading +3: the control
                      port is the indexed pointer/data port and reading it
                      toggles the pointer flip-flop.

=============================================================================
Write-only registers, read back  --  db fec5e000 4 / db fec5c000 4
=============================================================================
fec5e000 : ff ff ff ff      SNCR (snoop control).  Read 0x40 in the July 2026
                            probe, 0xff now -> reads are NOT meaningful.  The
                            manual says write-only; keep a software shadow.
fec5c000 : 80 ff e7 ff      ESR (enable slave select).  +0 = 80, as in July.

=============================================================================
NVRAM: the VIC init table RMON replays  --  db fec20000 20
=============================================================================
fec20000 : 00 ab f0 af 60 b3 40 57 10 a7 46 a3 00 b7 12 c3
fec20010 : 82 c7 16 cb 82 cf 00 d3 00 d7 00 db 00 df 80 7f

(value, VIC byte-offset) pairs:
    00->ab  f0->af  60->b3  40->57  10->a7  46->a3  00->b7  12->c3
    82->c7  16->cb  82->cf  00->d3  00->d7  00->db  00->df  80->7f

Two things this settles:
  * LIVBR (+57) is *configured* to 0x40 from NVRAM - it is not a ROM constant.
  * SSCR0 (+c3) is stored as 0x12 but reads back 0xd2, i.e. RMON ORs in the
    top two bits (tick enable + rate) at run time.  The kernel sees 0x12 at
    power-on and prints "E17: power-on VIC SSCR0 = 0x12 (RMON's value, before
    enable)" - consistent, because U-Boot clears the enable first.
  * +a3 reads 0x44 live but is 0x46 in the table, and +c7/+cf read 0x62 but
    are 0x82 in the table: the RE'd "special-casing of 0xc7/0xcf from
    fec700a8 bits 3-4" is real (a8 = 0x528b -> those bits = 01).

=============================================================================
Battery-backed RTC  --  db fec207f8 8
=============================================================================
fec207f8 : 08 37 02 00 05 11 09 56
    ctl=08 sec=37 min=02 hour=00 dow=05 date=11 month=09 year=56 (all BCD)
    -> 2026-09-11 00:02:37, and the kernel agrees ("setting system clock to
       2026-09-11T00:06:51 UTC"), so the year register is years-since-1970.
    The real date was 2026-09-21: the clock is TEN DAYS SLOW.  Since the NVRAM
    contents are intact the battery still holds data; the oscillator appears
    to lose time (or stop) while the board is powered down.  Worth a look.
